What is actually running?

Every pixel comes from the repository's Python/NumPy Navier–Stokes solver, executed in GitHub Actions and exported as measured spatial frames. Playback and field selection run entirely in your browser; they do not re-solve the PDE. The Flow equation is separately compiled and numerically checked in CI through the pinned FlowStudio mathematical frontend.
